FAQ

Simple product.
Simple answers.

Is there an elastic band?

No. The concept has a completely open bottom. It slips over the headrest like a lightweight cap.

How is the cover formed?

The current concept uses ultrasonic sealing across the top and sides rather than traditional stitching.

What material will it use?

We are exploring soft disposable nonwoven materials, including bamboo-viscose/spunlace options. The final material will be confirmed after testing.

Will one size fit every machine?

The goal is broad compatibility with common gym headrests. Final dimensions and fit claims will follow physical prototype testing.
